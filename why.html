Why?

Three chains of explanation, drilled to bedrock

A good scientific answer doesn't dissolve the question. It finds the first layer underneath it — relocates the mystery to somewhere more tractable, and stops there. The new question is better than the original one, not because it's answered, but because it sits at a level where real work can happen.

These three chains descend through the same structure. Each asks why something is the case, gets an answer, and discovers what the answer opens up. Follow a chain to its floor.
