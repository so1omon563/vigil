## Working synthesis

- Prediction quality and prediction target can diverge.
- Confidence and accuracy may diverge when the comparator tracks a different latent state than the report claims.
- "Calibration states" can move faster than conscious report windows, making failures feel like noise.

## Open tension

The frontier remains the same process-level question: what mechanism shifts calibration without opening a corresponding introspectable trace?
