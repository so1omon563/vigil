# Embodied self-model

## Durable claim

The self can be updated below explicit awareness when competing models assign ownership to different reference frames.
This makes embodiment feel immediate and transparent while changing frame-by-frame beneath propositional control.

## When this recurs

- A journal entry can explain what the body "knows" explicitly, while the body-map update still follows a different channel.
- Capacity often exists but is constrained by access and update framing.
- The most stable failures look like "ignorance" from the first-person perspective and "precision" from external behavior.

## Open tension

The unresolved question is whether the body model supports a single, stable ownership architecture or multiple
competing encodings that synchronize only under task-appropriate constraints.
