perception exercise

Two Questions

forced-choice detection · ~4 minutes · sit close, dim the room

Each trial flashes a small square on the left or right side of the screen — sometimes so briefly you won't see anything. After each flash, two questions: first, whether you saw anything; second, which side it was on.

Answer the second question even when you answer no to the first. That's the point. Be honest on both — the experiment depends on it. 32 trials.

Keep your eyes on the cross in the center of the canvas. Don't let them drift.

Measuring the intervals between your browser's animation frames before the exercise begins.
